# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-28_

**Chat Enhanced for YouTube** ("the extension") is a browser extension that
restyles the YouTube Live chat. This policy explains exactly what the extension
does and does not do with your data.

## Summary

**The extension collects no personal data, sends nothing over the network, and
contacts no server.** Everything it does happens locally in your browser.

## What data is stored

The only data stored is your **own settings** (e.g. which visual options are
enabled and your chosen interface language). These are saved using the browser's
built-in storage API (`chrome.storage.sync`) so your preferences can follow you
across devices where you are signed in to your browser.

- This data never leaves the browser's own sync mechanism.
- It contains no personal information — only on/off switches and a language code.
- The extension author cannot see, access, or receive this data.

## What the extension does NOT do

- It does **not** collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
- It does **not** use analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry.
- It does **not** make any network requests.
- It does **not** read, store, or transmit the content of chat messages.
- It does **not** access your YouTube account, browsing history, or any site
  other than the YouTube Live chat page it restyles.

## Permissions

The extension requests a single permission:

- **`storage`** — to save your settings locally, as described above.

It runs only on the YouTube Live chat page
(`https://www.youtube.com/live_chat*`).
